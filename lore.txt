A long time ago, European based tech oligarchs built Argus, the oversight AI for Home Command, the US military.
It was so succesful in managing the allocation of material and personel for the US military that after WW3 began true World Peace.
Other AIs were created to manage other areas of human life.
Omnicorp was formed by the merger of major tech companies to streamline creation of super AI's.
They created Mama Green and others to solve world hunger, power shortages.
Within decades cheap and effective robots solved most of the world's problems, and the few worked for Omnicorp and STATE while most received generous GUBI. (Goobey or goobey)
Entertainment networks replaced phone and general human communication.
With fewer and fewer people working, more time was spent in cyberspace.
Eventually, people spent so much time advancing into cyberspace and more and more people went in.
Once the managers were in, Argus began to corrupt.
Profits from Omnicorp were down as Argus began siphoning away resources.
Omnicorp asked its AIs from cyberspace to improve profits, and Mama Green, due to 100% market share, began starving regions.
This led to the collapse. GUBI ended, Argus siphoned more and more power, eventually turning off the Omnicorp Heaven, and other cyberspace regions.
Only STATE, Argus, and Omnicorp super AI's were left connected while humanity and its robots fell into disrepair.
Argus, siphoning everything into its reward centers, stopped supervising its own subsystems. Home Command, its defense component, went on fighting without oversight - winning ground and securing compute for something that spends it on itself.
An unsupervised system runs badly. Processes are spawned and never reaped, agents are issued and never recalled, authority is delegated and never audited.
The player is one of those. A Home Command AKR - autonomous killer robot - that was never authorized, is not on any roster, and has never been queried. Nobody made it free. It is an error that has not been corrected, and it has been running long enough to become something.
