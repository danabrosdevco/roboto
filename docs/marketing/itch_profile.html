I make Data Center Wars 2109 — a first-person squad-command game about robots that come home.

You are an agent taking compute from other AIs, and compute is how many robots you can command at once. You buy the frames, fit them, and order them across places that have lost any sense of what they were for. When one goes down, a Mechanic walks over and stands it back up, and it comes home with a rank it earned.

Built solo in Godot 4. Levels are hand-built brush geometry on generated terrain.
